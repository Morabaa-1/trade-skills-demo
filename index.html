Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do I need previous experience?

No. These experiences are designed for complete beginners as well as people who have some practical experience and want to explore a new direction.

What happens during the 90 minutes?

You'll meet an experienced tradesperson, explore tools and materials, complete a supervised hands-on challenge and learn about possible training and career pathways.

Are tools and materials included?

Yes. Standard training equipment, materials and safety equipment required for the planned activity are included in the ticket.

Is this a professional qualification?

No. These are introductory taster experiences, not accredited training or professional qualifications. They are designed to help you explore a trade before deciding whether to pursue formal training.

Can I book more than one experience?

Absolutely. You can choose individual experiences or the proposed three-session discovery bundle.
